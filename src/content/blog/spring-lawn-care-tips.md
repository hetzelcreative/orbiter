Spring is here, and it's time to get your lawn back in shape after a long Iowa winter. Here are five things every homeowner in the Glenwood area should do to set their lawn up for success this season.

## 1. Don't Rush the First Mow

Wait until your grass is actively growing and has reached about 3–4 inches before the first cut. Mowing too early can stress the turf and expose it to late-season frost.

## 2. Clean Up Debris

Remove any leftover leaves, sticks, and debris from winter. A clean lawn allows sunlight and air to reach the soil, which helps new growth get started faster.

## 3. Check for Bare Spots

Winter can leave behind thin or bare areas. Mark them now so you can overseed later in the spring or early fall for the best results.

## 4. Apply Pre-Emergent Weed Control

Timing is everything with pre-emergent herbicides. In Southwest Iowa, the ideal window is late March through mid-April — right when forsythia bushes start to bloom.

## 5. Schedule Your First Treatment

If you're on a lawn treatment program, your first application should go down in early to mid-April. This kickstarts the season with the right nutrients and weed prevention.
